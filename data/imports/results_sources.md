# Results Import Sources

`results.csv` contains completed group-stage scores imported from published match-score pages.

- SB Nation, "World Cup schedule 2026: How to watch every match, scores, and more", updated June 24, 2026. Used for completed scores from June 11 through June 23 listed in the group-stage schedule.
- The Guardian, "Colombia 1-0 DR Congo: World Cup 2026 - as it happened", published June 24, 2026. Used for Colombia 1-0 DR Congo.
- SB Nation, "2026 World Cup Standings: Full list of teams", updated June 28, 2026. Used for final group-stage scores from June 24 through June 27 and cross-checking all group standings.
- SB Nation, "World Cup Round of 32: Full schedule and scores", updated July 4, 2026. Used for Round of 32 scores from June 28 through July 3.
- The Guardian, "Switzerland 2-0 Algeria: World Cup 2026 last 32 - as it happened", published July 3, 2026. Used to verify Switzerland 2-0 Algeria after one Round of 32 schedule page displayed the opponent name inconsistently.
- SB Nation, "2026 World Cup Round of 16: Full schedule and scores", updated July 8, 2026. Used for Round of 16 scores from July 4 through July 7 and confirmed the eight quarter-finalists.
- SB Nation, "World Cup 2026: Quarterfinals matches, potential Semifinal fixtures", updated July 8, 2026. Used for confirmed quarter-final matchups and semifinal paths.
- FIFA/host-stadium schedule tables mirrored in venue pages for Boston Stadium, Los Angeles Stadium, Miami Stadium and Kansas City Stadium. Used for quarter-final match IDs, dates, venues and UTC kickoffs.

These rows are actual result imports, not regenerated predictions. They update standings only. They do not make completed matches eligible for model evaluation unless a pre-kickoff prediction snapshot exists.
